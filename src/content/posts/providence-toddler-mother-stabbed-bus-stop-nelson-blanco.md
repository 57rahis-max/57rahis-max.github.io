<p>A man is charged with stabbing a 2-year-old girl in her stroller and wounding her mother at a bus stop in Providence, Rhode Island, on Wednesday afternoon, Oct. 7, 2026, in what police called an unprovoked attack on strangers.</p>

<p>Nelson Blanco, 46, faces charges including assault with intent to commit murder, police said, according to <a href="https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/providence-ri-stabbing-update/4027891/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NBC Boston</a> and <a href="https://www.foxnews.com/us/toddler-stabbed-face-stranger-previously-deemed-unfit-trial-attacks-two-children-report" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Fox News</a>.</p>

<h2>What police say happened</h2>

<p>The mother and daughter were waiting near Broad and Public streets for the girl’s siblings to get off a school bus. Police Chief Oscar Perez said Blanco insulted the mother, then took out a knife and began stabbing the child. Police said he did not know either of them.</p>

<p>Bystanders intervened, and Blanco ran into the basement of a nearby home on Daboll Street, where officers arrested him, police said.</p>

<p>The girl was taken to Hasbro Children’s Hospital in critical condition and remained in intensive care after surgery, <a href="https://turnto10.com/news/local/bystanders-rushed-to-help-toddler-stabbed-at-providence-bus-stop-oct-8-2026" target="_blank" rel="noopener">WJAR</a> reported. Her mother was also stabbed; she was treated and released from the hospital.</p>

<h2>His past cases</h2>

<p>Blanco’s criminal record includes a 2003 case in which he was charged with stabbing two 7-year-old boys in Providence. He was found incompetent to stand trial in that case, according to NBC Boston and Fox News.</p>

<p>Blanco was arraigned on Thursday, Oct. 8, 2026.</p>

<p>The allegations have not been proven in court.</p>
