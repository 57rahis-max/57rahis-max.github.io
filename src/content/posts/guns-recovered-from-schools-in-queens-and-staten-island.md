<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On Monday morning (September 14, 2026), at Curtis High School in Staten Island, an 18-year-old high school student, Samuel Owusu, was caught with a loaded gun in his bag by school safety agents and was subsequently taken into police custody.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">According to reports, the student was fighting with another student on Hamilton Avenue in the St. George neighborhood around 10:20 AM when school safety agents intervened and broke up the fight. It was during this incident that they found the loaded gun in the backpack. The school reportedly does not have metal detectors.</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="576" src="/images/2026/09/guns-recovered-from-schools-in-queens-and-staten-island.jpg" alt="Handguns and firearms recovered in school weapons security operations" class="wp-image-74" srcset="/images/2026/09/guns-recovered-from-schools-in-queens-and-staten-island.jpg 1024w, /images/2026/09/guns-recovered-from-schools-in-queens-and-staten-island-300x169.jpg 300w, /images/2026/09/guns-recovered-from-schools-in-queens-and-staten-island-768x432.jpg 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Loaded firearms recovered during school security screening incidents in New York City.</figcaption></figure>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The statement released by the Department of Education read, &#8220;Weapons have no place in our schools or our city. The incident was quickly and safely addressed by our School Safety Agents, and no students or staff were injured. The gun was safely recovered after a search was conducted of the student’s bookbag. NYPD was called immediately upon discovery of the firearm.”</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The authorities revealed that the teen did not pull out the gun and no one was harmed during the incident. However, Owusu was charged with multiple gun possession charges and unlawful possession of cannabis. (More details on this incident can be found on <a href="https://www.univision.com/local/nueva-york-wxtv/estudiantes-enfrentan-cargos-armas-cargadas-escuelas-nueva-york?utm_source=gemini" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Univision</a>).</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Second Firearm Detected at Queens High School</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There was another similar incident at a high school in Queens on Monday (September 14, 2026), where another gun was found in a bag. Police revealed that the weapon was discovered when the bag went through a scanner. Contrary to earlier ambiguities, reports from <a href="https://rooseveltislanddaily.news/2026/09/16/loaded-firearm-discovered-during-security-screening-at-queens-high-school/?utm_source=gemini" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Roosevelt Island Daily News</a> confirm that the recovered firearm was indeed loaded.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Student Stabbed Outside Bronx Campus</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In another case, a 17-year-old student was stabbed outside Taft Educational Campus in the Bronx around 9:30 AM, while he was accompanied by his mother to register at a school. According to <a href="https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/student-stabbed-outside-bronx-school-police-say/?utm_source=gemini" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Gothamist</a>, the teen was approached by two suspects; one punched him in the face and another stabbed him. While the victim was taken to a hospital and is recovering, the suspects remain at large.</p>
