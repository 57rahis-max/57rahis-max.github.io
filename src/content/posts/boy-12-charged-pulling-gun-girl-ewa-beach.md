<p>A 12-year-old boy is facing firearms and terroristic threatening charges after police say he pulled a gun on a 13-year-old girl in Ewa Beach and threatened to shoot her brother.</p>

<p>It happened at about 7:40 a.m. on Thursday, September 17, 2026, near a business on Fort Weaver Road. The Honolulu Police Department says the boy walked up to the girl and brandished a firearm.</p>

<p>The girl reported it at school later. Staff there found a gun in the boy’s backpack and called police, <a href="https://www.khon2.com/hawaii-crime/ewa-beach-juvenile-arrested/">KHON2 reported</a>.</p>

<p>Officers found the boy, confirmed who he was and arrested him on suspicion of first-degree terroristic threatening and a firearms charge known as place to keep.</p>

<p>The next day, Friday, September 18, 2026, police charged him, and a third count was added. Alongside first-degree terroristic threatening and place to keep, he now faces a prohibited weapons charge, <a href="https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2026/09/19/12-year-old-charged-after-allegedly-pointing-gun-teen-ewa-beach/">Hawaii News Now reported</a>.</p>
<p>The allegations have not been proven in court.</p>

<p>Police say he was not charged as an adult. His name has not been released because he is a minor, and he was still in police custody as of Friday, <a href="https://www.kitv.com/news/crime/12-year-old-boy-charged-after-allegedly-pulling-gun-on-13-year-old-girl-in/article_e76d0be4-6451-4b8f-a882-f1b3bafccc56.html">according to KITV</a>.</p>

<p>No one was hurt. The investigation is continuing, and police have not released further details, including where the boy got the gun or which school he attends, <a href="https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/09/19/breaking-news/boy-12-charged-after-allegedly-pulling-gun-on-girl-in-ewa-beach/">the Honolulu Star-Advertiser reported</a>.</p>
