<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A 36-year-old man is facing a murder charge in connection with a shooting that killed a man in uptown Charlotte over Labor Day weekend, police say.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Charlotte-Mecklenburg police announced on Monday, Sept. 7, that detectives had identified Tyrell Deshawn Valentine as a suspect in the death of 33-year-old Manuel De Jesus Martinez, obtained warrants for his arrest, and taken him into custody in South Carolina, according to <a href="https://charlottenc.gov/CMPD/News-Releases/Pages/Homicide-Investigation-in-the-Central-Division-20260906-0345-02.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CMPD’s homicide investigation report</a>.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Police Say Happened on West Trade Street</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Officers were called to the 700 block of West Trade Street — near the Johnson &amp; Wales University campus — shortly before 4 a.m. Sunday, Sept. 6, for what was reported as an assault with a deadly weapon, according to CMPD.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When they arrived, they found a man with an apparent gunshot wound. Medic pronounced him dead at the scene. Police later identified him as Martinez and said his family had been notified.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Homicide detectives, Crime Scene Search investigators, and Victim Services staff all responded that morning.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Arrest Made in Union County, South Carolina</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Valentine was located and arrested without incident by the South Carolina Law Enforcement Division (SLED) and the Union County Sheriff’s Office in South Carolina, working alongside CMPD’s Violent Criminal Apprehension Team.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">CMPD detectives traveled there to interview him. Police say he’ll be extradited back to Mecklenburg County in the coming weeks and turned over to the Mecklenburg County Sheriff’s Office, where he faces a murder charge.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">One note on timing: CMPD’s own release lists the arrest date in a way that conflicts with itself, and local outlets have reported it differently as a result. What’s clear is that police announced the arrest on Sept. 7 — within roughly a day of the shooting.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Police Haven’t Said</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A lot, still. CMPD hasn’t released a motive, hasn’t described what led up to the shooting, and hasn’t said whether Martinez and Valentine knew each other. The department says the investigation is active and ongoing, and that more will be released as it develops.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Valentine has been charged, not convicted.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Anyone with information is asked to call CMPD’s Homicide Unit at 704-432-8477 and ask for a detective, or contact Crime Stoppers anonymously at 704-334-1600.</p>
