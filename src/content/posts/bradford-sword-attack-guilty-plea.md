<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A Bradford man has pleaded guilty to aggravated assault in the Bradford sword attack, admitting to stabbing another man with a sword on a city walking trail in May. Adrian Washington entered the plea on September 3 during a conference at the McKean County Courthouse in Smethport, Pennsylvania, and <a href="https://crimewatch.net/us/pa/mckean/da/136029/post/man-pleads-guilty-stabbing-man-sword-walking-trail" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">District Attorney Stephanie Vettenburg-Shaffer&#8217;s office announced the plea</a> the next day, a development also reported by <a href="https://fox56.com/news/local/man-pleads-guilty-to-stabbing-man-with-sword-on-bradford-walking-trail" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Fox56</a>.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Police Describe the Bradford Sword Attack</h2>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The case traces back to May 18, when a man walking a trail near Country Fair in Bradford got into an argument with Washington. Police say Washington left the area, then returned carrying a katana-style sword. A second confrontation followed, and Washington stabbed the man in the abdomen, according to the <a href="https://crimewatch.net/us/pa/mckean/city-of-bradford-pd/68048/arrests/washington-adrian-c-18-2702-a4-aggravated-assault-deadly-weapon-f1-and-2-additional" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">original police report</a>. The wound required an airlift to Penn Highlands DuBois for treatment, and officers arrested Washington two days later, on May 20.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Washington Pleads Guilty to Aggravated Assault</h2>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Police originally charged Washington with aggravated assault with a deadly weapon, terroristic threats and recklessly endangering another person. The district attorney&#8217;s September 4 announcement confirms that he pleaded guilty to aggravated assault.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Sentencing Scheduled for October 16</h2>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Washington is scheduled to be sentenced October 16, according to the district attorney&#8217;s office.</p>
