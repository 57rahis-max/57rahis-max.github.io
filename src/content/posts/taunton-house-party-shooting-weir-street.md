<p class="wp-block-paragraph">One person is dead and seven others were injured after a shooting at a house party in Taunton, Massachusetts, early Sunday, according to <a href="https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/taunton-massachusetts-shooting-weir-street/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CBS Boston</a> and <a href="https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/taunton-mass-shooting-fatal-multiple-people-injured/4010264/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">NBC Boston</a>, both citing Taunton Police and Bristol County District Attorney Thomas M. Quinn III. The fatality was confirmed in updated reporting after Taunton Police&#8217;s initial release on the shooting had said there were no known fatalities.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Officers Respond to Weir Street Home</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Taunton Police received several 911 calls around 12:30 a.m. Sunday, Sept. 6, reporting shots fired and multiple people shot in the area of Weir and Sumner streets, according to <a href="https://news.jgpr.net/2026/09/06/taunton-police-investigating-shooting-at-weir-street-house-party/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">a police release issued through John Guilfoil Public Relations</a>. Responding officers determined the shooting occurred at a home on Weir Street near its intersection with Sumner Street, where they located multiple gunshot victims.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Eight Shot, One Fatally</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Police identified eight shooting victims from the gathering, all in their early twenties, CBS Boston and NBC Boston reported. Zirovick Depina, 22, of Brockton, was pronounced dead early Sunday morning. Of the surviving victims, one is in serious but stable condition, and the remaining six suffered non-life-threatening injuries, according to Bristol County DA Thomas Quinn.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Multiple victims were taken to area hospitals by ambulance, police said. Investigators also learned that other victims had left the scene and sought medical treatment on their own, which is why the full victim count wasn&#8217;t established in Taunton Police&#8217;s initial release.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Local and State Police Investigate</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Massachusetts State Police Detective Unit assigned to the Bristol County District Attorney&#8217;s Office is investigating alongside Taunton Police, with the State Police Crime Scene Services Section assisting at the scene. CBS Boston reported that the DA&#8217;s office did not say whether any arrests had been made.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The investigation remains active. Anyone with information is asked to call the Taunton Police Department.</p>
