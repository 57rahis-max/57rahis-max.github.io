<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A dispute involving a basketball preceded a fatal shooting at&nbsp;<a href="https://hemisfair.org/">Hemisfair</a>, the downtown San Antonio park and event space, according to&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ksat.com/news/local/2026/09/03/affidavit-man-arrested-charged-with-murder-in-connection-to-shooting-at-hemisfair-park/">newly released arrest records</a>. Police have since charged a 23-year-old man with murder.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Happened at Hemisfair</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">San Antonio police responded to a shooting call just before 5 a.m. on Sunday, August 30, in the 600 block of Hemisfair Plaza Way, near South Alamo Street. Officers found 35-year-old Jonathan Alderete with a gunshot wound. He was taken to a hospital, where he later died.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Investigators said at the time that Alderete had been in a physical altercation with another man before the shooting, and that the fight appeared to involve a dispute over property. Police did not immediately identify a suspect.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Arrest Records Detail the Basketball Dispute</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">According to&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ksat.com/news/local/2026/09/03/affidavit-man-arrested-charged-with-murder-in-connection-to-shooting-at-hemisfair-park/">an arrest affidavit</a>, surveillance video from the area showed 23-year-old Isean Hardeman and three others picking up a basketball belonging to Alderete’s group and taking it to a nearby court. Investigators say that encounter escalated into the confrontation in which Alderete was shot. The affidavit does not explain what prompted the fight after they picked up the basketball.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The same surveillance footage helped investigators identify Hardeman as a suspect, according to the affidavit: he fled the scene on an e-scooter, ditched it, ran past a downtown hotel, and boarded a VIA bus.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Investigators later traced Hardeman to a South Side apartment. He was booked into the Bexar County Jail on September 3, four days after the shooting. He has been charged with murder, a first-degree felony, and is being held on $250,000 bond.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">An Ongoing Investigation</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Police have not said what led to the initial dispute over the basketball or why it turned violent. The case remains under investigation by San Antonio police.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Hemisfair, once the site of the 1968 World’s Fair, has been redeveloped in recent years into a downtown park and gathering space with a basketball court, event lawns and civic programming — making the shooting a jarring incident for a site more associated with families and public events than violent crime.</p>
